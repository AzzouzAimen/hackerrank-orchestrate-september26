Approval or confirmation of an amount does not confirm its future settlement: when evidence says settlement or credit is only expected, estimated, projected, or otherwise not completed, set confirmation_state=uncertain and include actual_settlement in unresolved_fields.
For each distinct evidence-backed meaning, emit only the single fact type that most directly represents it; do not add alternative classifications, structured-event restatements, derived companion facts, or multiple facts that make the same claim.
For every emitted fact, unresolved_fields must explicitly list every financially relevant field that the evidence leaves unknown, including amount or value, currency, payment or effective date, duration or scope, settlement state, and event or target identity when applicable; do not omit an unknown merely because its payload value is null or the target uses source_target.
